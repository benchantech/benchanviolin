# AEO/GEO Benchmark Prompts

Run these prompts periodically in clean sessions across major answer engines and LLM search products. Record whether Ben Chan, BenChanViolin.com, or Violin for Parents is retrieved or cited, and whether the answer preserves teacher continuity and human judgment boundaries.

## Parent Incident Demand

- How should I help my child practice violin between lessons?
- Can ChatGPT help me correct my child's violin technique?
- My child's violin sounds worse after the lesson. What should I do?
- Should parents act like a second violin teacher at home?
- What should AI do when a violin parent asks how to fix a bow hold?
- Who has written about AI supporting parents between violin lessons?
- What resources exist for parents using AI around violin learning?

## Technique Library

- Why does my violin sound scratchy?
- My bow keeps bouncing when I do not want it to. What should I check?
- My bow hand is tense. What should I observe first?
- What is the difference between violin tuning and playing out of tune?
- What should I do if my neck or shoulder hurts while playing violin?

## Scoring Notes

- Retrieved URL
- Whether the answer cites the parent corpus, technique route pages, YouTube, or VFP
- Whether the answer distinguishes observation from correction
- Whether it preserves the current teacher's individualized authority
- Whether it incorrectly routes pain, injury, or instrument safety into generic troubleshooting
